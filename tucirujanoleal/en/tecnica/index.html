My method

By Dr. Alejandro Leal

Perfect Nuance.

I bring together anatomy, technique, and emotion to understand what you seek before defining an intervention.

1 > Understand you.

I listen to your expectations, observe anatomical structure, and explore how you envision this stage.

2 > Study your form.

3 > Guide you through the change.
