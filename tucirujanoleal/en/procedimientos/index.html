What I do

A plan shaped around your anatomy.

I consider each procedure during an in-person consultation. I base that decision on your characteristics, expectations, and test results.

BodyBreast and buttocksFaceIn the office

Body.

My practice includes Lipo Quantum, liposculpture, muscle definition, abdominoplasty, brachioplasty, and combined post-pregnancy surgery.

4D liposculpture

I study fat distribution and muscle definition. I assess liposuction and fat transfer elsewhere according to your proportions.

Mommy Makeover

I combine procedures to address changes from pregnancy. I assess your abdomen, body contours, and breasts within this surgical plan.

Body contouring and definition

I analyze your contours and muscle definition. I integrate laser and radiofrequency according to the characteristics of your tissues.

Brachioplasty

I assess excess skin on your arms. I consider liposculpture and excision as part of the surgical plan.

Breast and buttocks.

I assess breast augmentation and a BBL.

Brazilian Butt Lift

I assess transferring fat from other areas to the buttocks. I study your tissues, proportions, and volume during the consultation.

Breast augmentation

I study your proportions. We assess implants or fat taken from another area of your body. Together, we define the scope during consultation.

Face.

I perform Full SMAS and Deep Plane facelifts. My practice also covers blepharoplasty, neck contouring, and otoplasty.

In the office.

My repertoire includes Morpheus 8 Signature, Liftera 2.0, Celebrity Face, and Absolute Facelift Turbo.

I define your plan and explain its cost during the consultation.
