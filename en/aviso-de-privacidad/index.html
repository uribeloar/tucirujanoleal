Caring for your data.

Updated: September 20, 2026.

Data controller.

Alejandro Enrique Leal Contreras, a board-certified plastic surgeon, is the data controller. He practices in Guadalajara, Jalisco, México.

Data and purposes.

The request collects your name, WhatsApp number, email address, city, country, planned investment, surgical interest, and preferred timeframe.

These details let us respond, guide the request, and coordinate your consultation. Investment and surgical interest each require separate express authorization.

Your planned investment is financial information.

Surgical interest may reveal sensitive information about your future health. Its processing requires express consent, given in writing or through electronic authentication.

During consultation, identification, medical history, examination findings, tests, and clinical images are collected under informed consent. Tax information is requested for invoicing.

Care includes consultation, authorized procedures, follow-up, instructions, and retention of the medical record. It also covers duly supported requests from competent authorities.

Additional choices.

You may consent to communications about services, reminders, surveys, and de-identified statistics. Choosing to opt out keeps your care available.

Consent for these purposes is managed separately. You may limit it through a written request to the controller.

Safekeeping and communication.

Administrative, technical, and physical safeguards protect data under confidentiality obligations. Its use is limited to the purposes described.

Technology, accounting, tax, or legal providers act as processors under the controller’s instructions and contractual obligations.

Transfers related to your care include healthcare personnel, hospitals, laboratories, and diagnostic imaging centers. They also include authorities acting on a substantiated demand, plus insurers at your request.

Each recipient assumes equivalent obligations. Additional transfers require prior notice and consent whenever legally applicable.

Each medical record is retained for a minimum period of five years following the final appointment. It is then restricted and deleted in accordance with applicable legal obligations.

Your rights.

You may access, rectify, cancel, or object to processing. You may also revoke authorizations and limit secondary uses.

Submit a written request to the controller. State your name, preferred response method, identification or accredited proof of representation, relevant data, and the right you wish to exercise. For rectification, provide correct information together with supporting documents.

A response is provided within twenty business days; an approved request takes effect during the following fifteen-day period. Each period may receive one justified extension of equal length.

Exercising these rights is free. Reasonable shipping or copying expenses may be charged.

Revocation applies going forward. Health, tax, and legal retention obligations remain in effect.

You may contact the competent personal data protection authority.

Browsing and changes.

Local storage remembers essential preferences in your browser. You can clear them in its settings.

Every update is published here with its date. Changes to purposes or transfers that require consent are communicated directly.
