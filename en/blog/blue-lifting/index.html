Blue Lifting

Endolifting with laser fiber under the skin.

“Blue Lifting is a minimally invasive procedure I perform outside the operating room, in the Liposser laser suite.”

What Blue Lifting is.

K Láser Blue is a multifunctional, multi-treatment platform with cutting-edge Italian technology; it ranks number 1 in the laser industry thanks to an exclusive design able to apply different wavelengths, which allows treatments across different fields of medicine, plastic surgery among them. This technology tightens more than 60% of the skin while respecting the tissue, minimally invasive, with better internal and external healing; it is also a cellular biostimulator.

I apply a few points of local anesthesia and use a very fine cannula (optical fiber) in subcision, that is, passing under the skin to create immediate tightening of your face and neck, lifting skin that has begun to sag in those areas, reshaping the zone and creating better facial harmony with a discreet finish. This procedure is known as endolifting.

Who is a candidate.

People with early or mild laxity and little fat. If there is significant laxity, excess localized fat and excess skin, I will propose another technique. It can be performed up to age 50, depending on the degree of facial aging.

Which areas.

Mainly the lower third of the face and the neck. Also arms, abdomen, inner thigh and above the knees.

What to expect afterwards.

The procedure takes about 1 hour per area. Skin retraction is immediate; the result reads best once the mild swelling of the first days subsides, from day 5 onward. Recovery is immediate and you can return to your routine the same day. A compression garment is worn and post-surgical rehabilitation sessions are done for edema and congestion, 5 to 10 sessions. The laser promotes collagen and elastin production: integral tightening, natural results and better skin quality.

The result.

A slimmer neck, a more defined jawline and a better balanced, rejuvenated face. It lasts approximately 3 to 5 years depending on lifestyle and weight; it can be repeated at 5 years. I recommend an annual consultation to correct spots and expression lines with stem cells, growth factors, botox or hyaluronic acid.
