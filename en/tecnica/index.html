The platforms I work with.

Morpheus8

It is the first and only device that treats fat accumulation by acting on subdermal tissue.

BodyTite

This innovative system uses the power of radiofrequency to sculpt your body precisely and safely.

FaceTite

One of the most advanced and safest facial rejuvenation and contouring procedures.

Accutite

An advanced, minimally invasive facial and body remodeling treatment.

K Láser Blue

A multifunctional, multi-treatment platform with cutting-edge Italian technology, number 1 in the industry.

Blue Lifting

It is a minimally invasive procedure with laser fiber under the skin, fast and effective, with minimal discomfort.

Where I use them.

I integrate these platforms within my protocols: Lipo Quantum RF and Lipo Total Definer with Ignite radiofrequency; Absolute Facelift Turbo with FaceTite Turbo, Morpheus8 Burst and K-Laser Blue; Celebrity Face with Liftera A2 and K Láser Blue; and Blue Lifting with the K Láser Blue fiber.

I choose the platform during the consultation, according to your tissues, your expectations and your test results.

I define your plan and explain the cost during the consultation.
