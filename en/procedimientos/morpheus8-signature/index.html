What Morpheus8 is.

Morpheus8 is the first and only device that treats fat accumulation by acting on subdermal tissue. It combines fractional radiofrequency with microneedle penetration, reaching the deepest layers of the skin.

It is an anti-aging treatment that rejuvenates the skin and stimulates collagen production. I use it to reduce cellulite and wrinkles, and also to treat hyperhidrosis, acne marks, stretch marks and other scars. Its results are visible from the first session and long-lasting.

FDA-approved technology, minimally invasive and fully effective: it remodels subdermal tissue by acting on the deepest layers of the skin.

What I achieve with Morpheus8.

Treating skin laxity · Skin rejuvenation and lifting · Cellulite reduction · Reduction of localized fat · Treating post-surgical fibrosis · Improving acne sequelae.

Its companion Morpheus8 Turbo adds a facial and body tightening effect, improved texture —smoother skin and tighter pores—, facial oval definition and luminosity. It is a certified InMode platform, and I am a certified expert in InMode technologies.
