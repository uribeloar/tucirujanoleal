My story

By Dr. Alejandro Leal

More than 20 years carving face and body.

I am a plastic surgeon and I practise plastic, aesthetic and reconstructive surgery. Here I tell you about my method, my background and my training.

My method: The Perfect Nuance.

“Across more than 20 years in plastic surgery, and as a pioneer of laser liposculpture and technology-assisted facial rejuvenation since 2008, I have refined a method of my own that brings together art, science and advanced technology to sculpt faces and bodies, bringing out the singular beauty of each of my patients.”

This method, which I called Perfect Nuance, is built around a whole transformation that reaches further than the physical. To achieve it I work from three pillars.

1 · Understanding the human being

I go further than a medical consultation: what matters most to me is knowing you, knowing the human being behind it, seeing your emotions and who you are competing with. The first thing I do is stand you in front of the mirror and ask you: what do you see? what do you feel? In that first conversation I explore your habits, your family and genetic history, and whether you are ready for that change in your life.

2 · Art, science, technology and precision

Then my focus is your anatomy. Your face or your body is like the stone I am going to carve. I study the factors that have changed it — genetic, nutritional and environmental — and I analyse it from the inside out: your bone structure, your muscle, your ligaments, the kinetics of your own movement and, last and most important, your skin. With all of that I build a surgical plan designed for you and choose the surgical technique and the technologies it calls for.

3 · Beyond surgery: your emotions

I consider emotional and psychological support essential throughout the whole process. A calm emotional and mental state is key to successful surgery and to a fast, effective recovery.

My background.

I am the inventor of the brachial suspension technique, for arm rejuvenation through minute incisions, since November 2005.

Together with Dr Víctor Mendoza Martínez I am a national pioneer of laser liposculpture in Mexico.

With him I created, worldwide, the techniques of laser gluteal firming, laser arm firming, laser facial endolifting, laser body skin firming and the definition of abdominals and obliques, pectorals, back, biceps, triceps and shoulders, for men and women.

Since 2008 I have been a pioneer in laser liposculpture and technology-assisted facial rejuvenation in Mexico and Latin America.

My training.

  • Gluteal augmentation, remodelling and firming. Miami, Florida. Fellowship with Dr Constantino Mendieta.
